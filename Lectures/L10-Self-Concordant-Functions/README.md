## Contents

- Gradient versus Newton's method: affine invariance, and the local norm &#8214;h&#8214;<sub>x</sub> as the fix for Kantorovich's result (Nesterov &amp; Nemirovski)
- Self-concordant functions: definition, elementary examples (&minus;log t, &minus;log det X, the second-order cone barrier) and boundary behavior
- Calculus of self-concordant functions: scaling, addition and affine transformations
- The Dikin ellipsoid and local quadratic upper and lower bounds; the Hessian varies smoothly
- The damped Newton method as a global descent method; the Newton decrement as a measure of suboptimality
- Quadratic convergence zone of the standard Newton method, and the overall Newton switching method
