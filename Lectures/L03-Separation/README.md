## Contents

- Dual spaces and linear functions; inner product spaces and the Riesz representation theorem
- Separation of convex sets by hyperplanes: proper, strict and strong separation
- Strong separation of compact convex sets; separation pitfalls
- The Hahn&ndash;Banach extension theorem; separating a point or a convex set from an open convex set
- Every closed convex set is an intersection of half-spaces
- Dual cones and their properties; the bipolar identity K** = K
- Farkas' lemma: certifying the infeasibility of a linear system
