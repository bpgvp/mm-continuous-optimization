# 7. Black-Box Optimization Methods

*Mon 19 Oct (wk 43)*

[&larr; Back to course homepage](https://bpgvp.github.io/mm-continuous-optimization/)

## Contents

- Performance of numerical methods: problem classes, oracles and accuracy (the black-box model)
- General iterative methods; analytical versus algorithmic complexity
- Lipschitz optimization over the unit box: the grid method and its accuracy
- Lower complexity bounds via a resisting oracle: global optimization is exponentially hard in the dimension
- Black-box convex optimization with a first-order (subgradient) oracle
- The bisection method in one dimension: upper and lower complexity bounds, and Nemirovski's hard function
- Cutting-plane methods and the center of gravity method; Gr&uuml;nbaum's pie-cutting theorem
- Complexity of the center of gravity method, O(n log(LD/&epsilon;)), and why it is not practical
