# 4. Lagrangian Duality

*Mon 28 Sep (wk 40)*

[&larr; Back to course homepage](https://bpgvp.github.io/mm-continuous-optimization/)

## Contents

- Duality as a bounding trick: combining constraints into certificates of optimality
- Generalized duality with nondecreasing price functions; conic constraints
- The Lagrangian and weak duality in four steps; minimax theorems and strong duality
- Sufficiently rich dual families; affine price functions and the classical Lagrangian
- Applications: top-k minimization and the support vector machine with the kernel trick
- Complementarity and saddle-point conditions; recovering a primal solution from the dual
- Constraint qualifications: conic duality, Slater's condition and its failure
