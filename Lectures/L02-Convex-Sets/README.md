# 2. Convexity

*Mon 14 Sep (wk 38)*

[&larr; Back to course homepage](https://bpgvp.github.io/mm-continuous-optimization/)

## Contents

- Linear, affine and convex sets; hulls and combinations; relative interior
- Extreme points and vertices; the Minkowski&ndash;Carath&eacute;odory theorem
- Standard convex sets: polyhedra, ellipsoids, norm balls and cones, the positive semidefinite cone
- Operations preserving convexity of sets: intersections, affine images and preimages
- Proper cones and generalized inequalities; minimum and minimal elements; multiobjective optimization
- Convex functions and extended values; epigraphs and sublevel sets
- Common convex functions and operations preserving convexity of functions
- Continuity and directional differentiability of convex functions; first-order optimality
