*Mon 5 Oct (wk 41)*

## Contents

- Recap of directional, G&acirc;teaux and continuous differentiability; stationary points
- Gradient descent; smooth functions with Lipschitz continuous gradients and dual norms
- Convergence of gradient descent with a fixed step size, and the optimal step size 1/L
- Exact line search, the Wolfe conditions and backtracking
- Subgradients and the subdifferential of a convex function; first-order optimality
- Supporting hyperplanes of the epigraph; the subdifferential is nonempty
- Conjugate functions, support functions and characteristic functions
- The support function of the subdifferential is the directional derivative; differentiability as a singleton subdifferential
- Conjugates and subdifferentials: g &isin; &part;f(x) if and only if x &isin; &part;f*(g)
