# 6. KKT Optimality Conditions

*Mon 12 Oct (wk 42)*

[&larr; Back to course homepage](https://bpgvp.github.io/mm-continuous-optimization/)

## Contents

- Recap of unconstrained optimality conditions; why vanishing gradients fail with constraints
- Subdifferential calculus: the sum rule and the subdifferential of a maximum (Danskin's theorem)
- The Karush&ndash;Kuhn&ndash;Tucker theorem for differentiable functions under the linear independence constraint qualification (LICQ)
- A mechanical interpretation of the KKT conditions: forces exerted by walls on a ball
- The KKT theorem for convex functions under Slater's condition, with a proof using subdifferentials
- Examples where LICQ or Slater's condition fails and no KKT multipliers exist
- Complementarity, and how a KKT point certifies strong duality
- Lagrangian mechanics: a mass&ndash;spring system as an energy minimization problem
